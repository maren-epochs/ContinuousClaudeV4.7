# Meeting background and agenda

How Amazon runs a narrative meeting, as described by Bezos (2017 shareholder letter) and former Amazonians: there is no presentation. The memo is handed out at the start (not sent ahead, so everyone reads the same version with the same attention), the room reads in silence, "in a kind of study hall", then discusses. Readers mark comments as they read. The author answers questions rather than walking through the memo. The most senior person usually speaks last so the discussion is not anchored on their view. The meeting ends with decisions, owners and dates written down. Reading time is roughly 3 minutes per page; a full six-pager takes 20-30 minutes.

Write `<slug>-meeting.md` with the sections below. Keep it to one page; it supports the memo and never repeats its argument.

## Template

```markdown
# <Memo title>: review meeting

**Date / length:** <date or [TBD]>, <60 or 90> minutes
**Decision requested:** <one sentence, identical to the memo's ask>
**Decider:** <name or role>

## Background

<2-4 sentences: why this meeting, what the memo covers, what the readers are asked to do at the end. No argument from the memo.>

## Attendees and roles

| Role | Who | Responsibility in the room |
|---|---|---|
| Decider | | Reads, asks, speaks last, makes or defers the call before the meeting ends |
| Author / owner | | Hands out the memo, answers questions, does not present or re-read the memo aloud |
| Readers | | Read in silence, mark comments and questions, raise the most important ones first |
| Note-taker | | Records decisions, open questions, action items with owner and date; reads them back at the end |
| <Subject expert, if needed> | | Answers questions on <area> |

## Agenda

| Time | Step | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | Open | Author states the decision requested in one sentence. No presentation. Memo handed out (printed or a shared doc opened at the same moment) |
| 0:03-0:<03+3×pages> | Silent reading | Everyone reads the whole memo, appendix as needed, and writes comments in the margin or as doc comments. Nobody talks |
| next ~45% of the remaining time | Discussion | Biggest questions first, then page order. Readers ask; author answers or records "we don't know, will find out by <date>". Decider speaks last |
| next ~20% | Decision | Decider decides, decides with conditions, or names what is needed to decide and by when |
| last 5 min | Read-back | Note-taker reads back decisions, owners and dates; author collects all written comments |

## Ground rules

- No slides, no presenting the memo; questions start after everyone has finished reading
- Comments go on the memo so the author keeps them all, including the ones there was no time to discuss
- "I don't know" is an acceptable answer when it comes with an owner and a date
- Disagree in the room, then commit to the decision

## Advance note to attendees (include when the group is new to the format)

<4-6 sentences, ready to send a few days before: there are no slides; a memo of about N pages will be handed out at the start and everyone reads it in silence for about M minutes; do not expect to receive it in advance; bring questions to the discussion, and mark comments on the memo as you read; the meeting ends with a decision or a dated plan to reach one.>
```

Scale the times to the memo: a 3-page decision memo fits 45 minutes (10 reading, 25 discussion, 10 decision and read-back); a full six-pager needs 60-90. State the computed reading time from the memo's page estimate.
