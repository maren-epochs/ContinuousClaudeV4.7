# Support org - FY26 review notes (raw, from Priya's planning doc)

Team: Larkspur customer support. We own all inbound support for the invoicing product (email, chat, in-app). 52 agents at end of FY26 in two hubs (Leeds, Manila), 6 team leads, support ops team of 4 (tooling, QA, workforce mgmt). We report to the COO.

## FY26 goals we set (from last year's OP1)
1. Median first response under 4 hours by Q4 -> got to 6.1. Missed.
2. CSAT 90% by Q4 -> 89% Q4. Close miss.
3. Self-service deflection 30% by Q4 -> 25%. Missed.
4. Keep cost per ticket under $55 -> hit every quarter ($42.3-$51.9).
5. Launch 24/5 chat coverage by Q2 -> launched in May (Q2). Hit.

## What happened
- March billing release (new tax engine) created a flood: ~11,200 tickets in 6 weeks tagged "tax-calc". Backlog went from 640 to 2,150. Q1 first response 12.8h, worst ever.
- We hired 7 contractors via a BPO in Q2 to burn down. Worked but quality was rough, CSAT on BPO-handled tickets 78% vs 86% in-house. We ended the BPO in Q3 and converted 3 of them to full-time.
- Attrition: 11 agents left in FY26 (21% annualized). Manila attrition 28%, Leeds 14%. Exit interviews: pay in Manila below market by ~12%, and "too much repetitive tax tickets".
- Self-service: help center rewrite shipped in Q3, deflection 18% -> 22% -> 25%. In-app guided fixes for the top 3 ticket types are designed but not built - product eng had no capacity.
- Top 5 ticket types = 46% of volume. #1 is "invoice not sent / delivery failure" (14% of volume), mostly caused by customer email domains rejecting our sender; product fix (dedicated sending domain) was scoped at 6 eng-weeks and never prioritized.
- Tickets per 1k accounts went UP from ~2,600 to ~2,900-3,300 since the tax release - volume is growing faster than accounts.

## FY27 asks / ideas (not final)
- Finance plan says accounts grow ~16% to ~12,800 by end of FY27.
- Option 1: hire to keep pace: +9 agents (~$540K/yr loaded, Leeds/Manila mix).
- Option 2: +4 agents ($240K) + fund in-app guided fixes and the sending-domain fix: need 1 product eng team for one quarter (product says that's roughly $310K of eng time) - our estimate is it removes 20-25% of volume.
- Manila pay adjustment to market: $96K/yr.
- Goal ideas: first response 4h by Q2 FY27, CSAT 91%, deflection 35%, tickets per 1k accounts back under 2,600.
- Priya's view: Option 2 + pay adjustment. COO is skeptical of "deflection math" since we missed 30% this year.
