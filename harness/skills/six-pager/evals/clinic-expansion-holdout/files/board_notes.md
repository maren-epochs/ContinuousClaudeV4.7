# Northgate Physio - 4th clinic question (notes for the March board)

We are a three-clinic physiotherapy group (owner-run, ~30 staff). The board asked whether to open a 4th clinic in Ashby (pop. ~62,000, 14 miles from Harrowgate Road) in FY27.

- Lease offered on a unit in Ashby: £48,000/yr, 10-year lease, break at year 5. Fit-out quote £185,000 (6 rooms). Landlord gives 6 months rent-free if we sign by 30 April.
- Harrowgate Road is full: 19-day wait for new patients, we turn away an estimated 30-40 self-pay enquiries a week (front desk tally over 4 weeks: 142 turned away). About a fifth of Harrowgate patients give an Ashby postcode (from our booking system, FY26).
- Castle Quay (opened 2022) is still losing money: 64% utilization, -£41K operating profit. It took longer than planned; plan said break-even in year 2. Main issue was hiring - we ran with 4.5 FTE for most of FY25 because we could not recruit. Recruitment is the bottleneck: average time to hire a band-6-equivalent physio this year was 14 weeks; two offers declined over pay.
- Average revenue per visit ~£72 across the group. Physio salary ~£44K + 20% on-costs.
- Bank will lend up to £250K at 7.5% over 5 years, or we fund from reserves (£410K cash, board wants ≥£250K kept as buffer).
- Alternative ideas raised: (a) add 2 rooms at Harrowgate Road by converting the back office (~£60K, planning permission needed, 3-4 months), (b) extend hours at Harrowgate to 8pm and Saturdays, (c) fix Castle Quay first and revisit Ashby in FY28.
- MD (Sarah) wants Ashby. Finance director (Tom) worried about running two loss-making clinics at once.
